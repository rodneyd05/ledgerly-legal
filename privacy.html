Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Ledgerly ("the app") is built and maintained by Rodney Decena ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your financial data stays on your device

Everything you enter in Ledgerly, such as accounts, balances, expenses, bills, debts, categories and settings, is saved in a database on your phone. The app has no cloud database and does not send this data to me or to anyone else. Each signed-in account has its own separate database on the device, so another account on the same phone cannot see it.

Signing in with Google (Android)

On Android you sign in with your Google account. This uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, services provided by Google. When you sign in, Google and Firebase process your Google account identifier, email address, display name and basic technical data such as IP address and device information, in order to verify who you are. The app uses your account identifier only to name and separate your local database, and shows your email on the Settings screen. I do not use this information for advertising and do not sell it.

Your use of Google services is also covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

iOS

The iOS version does not currently have sign-in. It does not collect any personal information.

What I do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No analytics or tracking inside the app.

Backups and deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, the data is deleted and I cannot recover it. Depending on your phone settings, Android's system backup may include app data in your own Google backup; that is controlled by you and Google, not by me.

To delete your sign-in record from Firebase Authentication, email me at the address below and I will remove it.

Children

Ledgerly is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date above. If the app later adds cloud sync, this policy will be updated before it does.
