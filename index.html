Ledgerly

Ledgerly is a small, offline-first money manager for Android and iOS. It keeps track of your savings, expenses, credit cards and IOUs in one place.

Your financial records are stored on your own device. Ledgerly only uploads them if you buy Premium on Android, as a private cloud backup that you can restore on a new phone.
