Delete Your Ledgerly Account

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Ledgerly account and the data linked to it. Ledgerly is made by Rodney Decena.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (Android)

  1. Open Ledgerly and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings, then find the Plan_and_data.cfg section.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap Yes, delete to confirm.
  4. If Google asks you to confirm it is you, choose your account again. This is a security step before an account can be deleted.

The deletion happens right away. You are returned to the sign-in screen.

Option 2: Ask me to delete it

If you can no longer open the app, email rodney88decena@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Ledgerly account". I will delete your sign-in record and reply to confirm, normally within 14 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication: your Google account identifier, email address and display name.
  • Your Ledgerly data on your phone (accounts, balances, expenses, bills, debts, categories and settings) when you delete in the app. If you use Option 2, uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove this data. It exists only on your device.

What is kept

Nothing is kept by me. Ledgerly has no server database, so there is no copy of your financial data to keep or delete, and I hold no retention period for it. Google may keep limited technical logs for security as described in Google's Privacy Policy, and your own Google account is not affected. Ads shown in the app are provided by Google AdMob, which is covered by Google's policies.
